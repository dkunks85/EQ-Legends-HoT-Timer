EQ Legends Companion v1.4.3 — Simplified Buff Setup

Changes:
- Fade Pattern removed from Spell Setup.
- Normal buffs now expire only from the manually entered M:SS timer.
- Landing Pattern is split into:
    Self landing
    Target landing (name implied)

Examples:

Self landing:
    You feel the spirit of wolf enter you.

Target landing:
    is surrounded by a brief lupine aura.

The app internally saves the target form as:
    {target} is surrounded by a brief lupine aura.

Users do not type {target} or || anymore.

HoT behavior is unchanged:
- Automatic HoT detection remains.
- Ranked HoT duration learning remains.
