EQ Legends Companion v1.5.2 — Colored HoT Bars

HoT colors:
- Druid: green
- Shaman: blue
- Cleric: gold

Nature warning:
- Druid and Shaman cards begin a smooth red pulse at 5.0 seconds.
- The left accent, countdown, progress fill, and card background pulse.
- Cleric Echo timers do not flash red.
- Manual buffs remain neutral.

The native WinForms ProgressBar was replaced with a custom colored fill panel
so each HoT family can have a reliable color on all Windows themes.
