EQ Legends Companion v1.5 — Cleric Echo HoTs

Added automatic Cleric HoT support:
- Echo of Health (level 8)
- Echoing Light (level 14)
- Renewing Echo (level 24)
- Celestial Echo (level 34)
- Sacred Echo (level 43)

Confirmed log forms:
- Self landing text:
    You are embraced by a spirit of healing.
- Immediate target-identifying heal:
    You healed Cosmos for 3 (51) hit points by Echo of Health.
- HoT tick:
    You healed Cosmos over time for 9 (20) hit points by Echo of Health.
- Fade:
    The echo of healing fades away.

Behavior:
- The immediate heal identifies the target and starts the timer.
- Over-time ticks use the existing synchronization logic.
- The targetless fade ends the newest active Cleric Echo timer.
- Observed duration is learned separately for exact ranked spell names.
- Existing spells.json files are automatically upgraded with the Cleric family.
