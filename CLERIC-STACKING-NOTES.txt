EQ Legends Companion v1.5.1 — Cleric stacking and no-damage handling

HoT slots:
- Nature: Druid + Shaman share one slot per target.
- Cleric: Cleric Echo uses a separate slot per target.
- Nature and Cleric can coexist on the same target.

Cleric behavior:
- Cast immediately starts a provisional timer:
    Echo of Health — Unknown target
- Self landing:
    You are embraced by a spirit of healing.
  assigns the timer to the local character.
- Immediate healing:
    You healed Cosmos for 38 (51) hit points by Echo of Health.
  confirms or corrects the target.
- If no damage is available, the provisional timer remains Unknown target
  unless a self-embrace line identifies the local character.
- The line:
    The echo of healing fades away.
  is ignored because it is only the short visual echo ending.
- The configured/rank-learned duration expires the actual HoT timer.

Nature behavior:
- A Shaman HoT replaces a Druid HoT on the same target and vice versa.
- Cleric Echo remains active beside it.
